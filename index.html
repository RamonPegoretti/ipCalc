ipCalc

IPv4 and IPv6 subnet calculator. Type IPs in decimal (IPv4), hex (IPv6) or binary; IPv4 results are in decimal and binary. Starting Python…
Fill in any two boxes and leave the rest empty, e.g. IP host + mask, network + number of hosts, or network + broadcast. Decimal or binary, like 11000000.10101000.00001010.01100001.
Either box can be decimal or binary.
Accepts 178, 10110010, 192.168.1.1 or a dotted binary address. Binary means exactly 8 zeros/ones per octet.
Subnet counts round up to a power of two: asking for 5 subnets gives 8. Hosts per subnet leaves room for the network and broadcast addresses.
Also accepts 50, 20, 10. The biggest subnets are placed first so every block lines up with no gaps.
Accepts short or full form (2001:db8::1, 2001:0db8:0000:…), address/64 in one box, or binary: eight groups of 16 bits. Results are in standard hex.
